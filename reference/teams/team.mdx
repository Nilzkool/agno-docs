The `Team` class provides a framework for coordinating multiple `Agent` instances or other `Team` instances to accomplish complex tasks. It manages inter-agent communication, shared context, and overall session flow. When configuring `Team` with a storage backend, ensure the storage is initialized with `mode="team"` to correctly manage team sessions.
